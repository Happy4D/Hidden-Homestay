Terms & Conditions

01 Payment

Bookings are confirmed by full advance payment via KHQR — scan the code at checkout with any Cambodian banking app (ABA, ACLEDA, Wing, Bakong…). Rates are quoted in USD per hour. Please keep your payment receipt.

02 Booking Changes

Need a different time? Reschedule at least 3 hours before your start time and we will move your booking at no charge (once per booking), subject to availability.

03 Cancellations

Cancel more than 24 hours before your start time for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours are refunded at 50%. Missed bookings without notice are non-refundable.

04 Additional Hours

Want to stay longer? Extra hours are charged at your room's hourly rate and depend on availability — just message our team before your session ends.

05 Property Damage

Guests are responsible for the room during their booking. Any accidental damage will be assessed fairly and charged at actual repair cost.

06 Guest Responsibilities

Please book with your real name and contact number, bring a valid ID at check-in, and respect the maximum occupancy of your room.

07 Personal Belongings

We take great care of your things, but Hidden Homestay cannot be held liable for lost or damaged personal items. Please take valuables with you when you leave.

08 House Rules

No smoking indoors, no loud parties, and no pets unless agreed in advance. Treat the room like your own — kindly leave it as you found it.
